# Topic 3.5 · Energy Sources, Storage & Efficiency
**Specification Strand:** 3. Implications of Wider Issues

## Energy Storage Technologies

- **Lithium-ion (NMC):** High specific energy density (~250 Wh/kg), nominal 3.7V per cell, high energy-to-weight; requires Battery Management System (BMS) for thermal runaway prevention.
- **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 / LFP):** Extremely stable chemistry, high thermal runaway ceiling, >3000 charge cycles, 3.2V nominal; ideal for stationary energy and robust EVs.
- **Supercapacitors:** Electrostatic double-layer storage; ultra-rapid charge/discharge, virtually unlimited cycle life, low specific energy; handles regenerative braking burst loads.

## Energy Transmission & Efficiency

- **Energy efficiency formula:** $\\text{Efficiency } \\eta = \\frac{\\text{Useful Output Power / Energy}}{\\text{Total Input Power / Energy}} \\times 100\\%$.
- **I^2R transmission loss:** Electrical conductor resistive power loss scales with the square of current ({loss} = I^2 R$). High-voltage transmission reduces current and cuts transmission heat losses.
- **Energy harvesting:** Piezoelectric vibration harvesting, Peltier-Seebeck thermoelectric generators, and micro photovoltaic cells powering autonomous low-energy IoT nodes.
