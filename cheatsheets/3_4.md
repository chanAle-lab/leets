# Topic 3.4 · Distribution Logistics & Intellectual Property
**Specification Strand:** 3. Implications of Wider Issues
**Syllabus Focus:** Distribution logistics, packaging optimization, Intellectual Property: patents, registered designs, trademarks, copyright, design rights

---

## Freight Logistics & Packaging Engineering

- **Volumetric cargo efficiency:** Sizing primary and secondary packaging to interlock without overhang on standard EUR pallets (1200 × 800 mm) or ISO containers (20ft / 40ft).
- **Transport modes & emissions:** Maritime shipping (lowest $ per tonne-km) vs rail, road freight, and air freight (highest carbon emissions and cost per kg).
- **Sustainable packaging:** Corrugated single/double wall board (high compressive crush strength, biodegradable) replacing expanded polystyrene (EPS) with moulded pulp or mushroom mycelium.

## Intellectual Property (IP) Law

- **Patents:** Grants up to **20 years** legal monopoly over novel technical inventions, mechanical movements, or industrial processes. Must possess novelty, inventive step, and industrial applicability.
- **Registered Designs:** Protects aesthetic visual appearance, shape, contours, and decorative ornamentation for up to **25 years** (renewed every 5 years).
- **Trademarks:** Protects distinctive brand names, logos, symbols, and slogans indefinitely, subject to 10-year renewal fees.
- **Copyright:** Automatic legal protection for original technical drawings, schematics, source code, and user manuals (author's life + 70 years).
- **Unregistered Design Rights:** Protects 3D functional shape automatically in the UK for 10 years from first commercial sale (or 15 years from creation).
