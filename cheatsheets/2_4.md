# Topic 2.4 · Product Lifecycles & Marketing Models
**Specification Strand:** 2. Existing Products & Practice
**Syllabus Focus:** Product life cycle stages (introduction, growth, maturity, decline), lifecycle extension strategies, digital marketing & social media models

---

## Product Life Cycle (PLC) Dynamics

- **Introduction phase:** High development & tooling cost amortization, low sales volume, negative cash flow; requires targeted marketing to innovators.
- **Growth phase:** Rapid market adoption, unit production costs drop via manufacturing economies of scale, distribution channels expand.
- **Maturity phase:** Peak sales, market saturation, intense competitor price pressure; cash generation peaks and funds subsequent R&D pipelines.
- **Decline phase:** Falling sales due to technological obsolescence or shifting consumer habits; leads to product discontinuation or harvest strategy.

## Extension Strategies & Digital Models

- **Lifecycle extension strategies:** Re-engineering aesthetic facelifts, software feature upgrades, entering emerging geographic markets, or re-targeting alternative commercial applications.
- **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) & viral models:** Bypassing traditional retail margins through digital channels, influencer unboxings, and community crowdfunding feedback.
