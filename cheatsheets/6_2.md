## Kinematics & Motion Conversion

- **Four motion regimes:** Rotary (continuous 360° circular), Linear (continuous straight-line single direction), Reciprocating (repetitive back-and-forth linear), Oscillating (repetitive back-and-forth arc around fixed pivot).
- **Rack and pinion:** Transforms rotary motion into precise linear motion (or vice-versa; CNC machine beds, automotive steering racks).
- **Cams and followers:** Shaped cam plate (eccentric, pear, snail) rotates to impart programmed displacement to follower (knife-edge, roller, flat-faced); characterized by Dwell, Rise, and Fall phases.
- **Mechanical linkages:** Bell crank (redirects linear force through 90° angle), Four-bar linkage (crank-rocker, double-crank), Pantograph (parallel motion scaling).

## Gear Calculations & Mechanical Advantage

- **Gear Ratio / Velocity Ratio:** $\\text{GR} = \\frac{\\text{Teeth on Driven (Output)}}{\\text{Teeth on Driver (Input)}} = \\frac{\\omega_{in}}{\\omega_{out}} = \\frac{N_{in}}{N_{out}}$.
- **Compound gear train:** Intermediate shafts bearing two rigidly keyed gears; total ratio is the product of individual gear stages: $\\text{GR}_{total} = \\text{GR}_1 \\times \\text{GR}_2 \\times \\dots$
- **Worm and worm wheel:** Achieves huge single-stage reduction (e.g. 50:1) with non-intersecting shafts at 90°; inherently self-locking, preventing output back-driving.
- **Epicyclic (Planetary) gearing:** Coaxial high-torque system comprising Sun gear, Planet gears on a Planet Carrier, and Annulus (ring gear); compact form factor for automatic transmissions.
- **Mechanical Advantage (MA):** $\\text{MA} = \\frac{\\text{Load (Output Force)}}{\\text{Effort (Input Force)}}$.
- **Velocity Ratio (VR):** $\\text{VR} = \\frac{\\text{Distance moved by Effort}}{\\text{Distance moved by Load}}$.
- **Efficiency:** $\\text{Efficiency } \\eta = \\frac{\\text{MA}}{\\text{VR}} \\times 100\\%$. Due to friction and inertia, $\\text{MA} < \\text{VR}$ and $\\eta < 100\\%$.
