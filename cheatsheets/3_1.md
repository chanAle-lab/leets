## Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) & Environmental Footprint

- **Life Cycle Assessment (LCA):** Standardized ISO 14040 methodology evaluating total environmental impact across 5 phases: 1. Raw Material Extraction -> 2. Material Processing -> 3. Manufacture -> 4. Operational Use -> 5. End of Life (EOL).
- **Embodied energy:** The cumulative non-renewable primary energy consumed from mining and refining raw materials to finished assembly.
- **Carbon footprint:** Total equivalent carbon dioxide ($) emissions generated across the entire product lifecycle.
- **Circular economy:** Regenerative industrial framework replacing linear 'take-make-dispose' with closed-loop biological and technical nutrient loops (Ellen MacArthur Foundation).

## Planned Obsolescence & Statutory Directives

- **Planned obsolescence types:** Technological (incompatible communication ports/software), functional (glued-in batteries, artificial component wear limits), and psychological (stylistic dating).
- **RoHS Directive:** Restriction of Hazardous Substances; strictly restricts hazardous materials in electronic products (Lead Pb, Mercury Hg, Cadmium Cd, Hexavalent Chromium Cr(VI), PBB, PBDE).
- **WEEE Directive:** Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment; legally mandates OEM take-back, treatment, and recycling of post-consumer electronic waste.
