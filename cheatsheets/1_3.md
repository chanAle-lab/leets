# Topic 1.3 · Usability, Inclusivity & Ergonomics
**Specification Strand:** 1. Identifying Requirements
**Syllabus Focus:** Usability analysis, inclusive design, anthropometric data (5th, 50th, 95th percentiles), user comfort, control layouts, UI design

---

## Anthropometrics & Percentile Design Rules

- **Anthropometry:** The measurement of physical dimensions, reach boundaries, biomechanical leverage, and strength capacities of human populations.
- **5th to 95th percentile envelope:** Standard engineering rule to accommodate at least 90% of the target user population.
- **Reach dimensions (5th percentile):** Operational reach, button distances, and shelf heights must be accessible to smaller users; designed according to the **5th percentile female** data.
- **Clearance dimensions (95th percentile):** Hand clearance, legroom, door apertures, and handle openings must fit larger users; designed according to the **95th percentile male** data.
- **Design for adjustability:** Incorporating mechanical sliders, telescopic linkages, or height adjustment mechanisms to comfortably span across the entire 5th to 95th percentile spectrum.

## Ergonomics & Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI)

- **Physical ergonomics:** Optimising grip diameters (power grip 30–45 mm; precision grip 8–15 mm), reducing repetitive strain injury (RSI) risks, and maintaining neutral wrist postures.
- **Cognitive ergonomics:** Minimising mental workload, preventing control confusion, intuitive layout mapping (e.g. clockwise rotation increases output), and sensory confirmation feedback within 100ms.
- **Inclusive design:** Creating accessible products that accommodate individuals with varying physical, sensory, or cognitive capabilities without requiring stigmatising adaptations (BS 8300 / Equality Act 2010).
