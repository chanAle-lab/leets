**Specification Strand:** 5. Material & Component Considerations

## Metals & Engineering Alloys

- **Mild Steel:** Ferrous; 0.15–0.30% Carbon. Ductile, highly weldable, tough; structural beams, car chassis, universal steel sections.
- **High Carbon Steel / Tool Steel:** Ferrous; 0.60–1.40% Carbon. Very hard, can be hardened and tempered; cutting blades, drill bits, stamping dies.
- **Cast Iron:** Ferrous; 2.0–4.0% Carbon. High compressive strength, brittle, superb vibration dampening; machine beds, engine blocks.
- **Aluminium:** Non-ferrous; low density ($\\rho \\approx 2700 \\text{ kg/m}^3$), natural oxide passivation layer resists corrosion, high thermal/electrical conductivity; aircraft skins, heatsinks.
- **Brass:** Copper-Zinc alloy (typically 70/30 or 60/40); low friction coefficient, acoustic resonance, corrosion resistant; valves, bearings, electrical pin connectors.

## Polymers (Thermosoftening & Thermosetting)

- **ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene):** Thermosoftening; copolymer combining chemical resistance (A), impact toughness (B), and structural rigidity (S); injection-moulded tool housings, 3D printing, consumer electronics.
- **HIPS (High Impact Polystyrene):** Thermosoftening; lightweight, rigid, easily thermoformed and vacuum formed; internal refrigerator liners, packaging.
- **Polypropylene (PP):** Thermosoftening; exceptional fatigue resistance to repeated flexing; integral living hinges, chemical containers.
- **Epoxy Resin:** Thermosetting; highly crosslinked polymer matrix, exceptional adhesive strength, chemical resistance, electrical insulator; composite matrix (CFRP), PCB FR4 substrate.
- **Polyester Resin:** Thermosetting; cured with MEKP catalyst; matrix for Glass-Reinforced Plastic (GRP), boat hulls, fairings.

## Composites, Smart & Modern Materials

- **CFRP (Carbon Fibre Reinforced Polymer):** Continuous high-tensile carbon fibres embedded in rigid epoxy resin matrix; exceptional strength-to-weight ratio and stiffness; aerospace spars, Formula 1 chassis.
- **GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic):** Woven or chopped glass fibres in polyester/epoxy matrix; lightweight, non-conductive, corrosion resistant; architectural cladding, marine hulls.
- **Shape Memory Alloys (SMA - Nitinol):** Nickel-Titanium alloy that returns to a memorized shape upon heating past its martensite-to-austenite transition temperature; medical arterial stents, robotic micro-actuators.
- **Modern Super-alloys & Graphene:** Inconel (nickel-chromium superalloy maintaining extreme strength at >1000°C in jet turbine blades); Graphene (single 2D layer of carbon atoms arranged in hexagonal lattice, tensile strength >130 GPa, ballistic thermal conductivity).
