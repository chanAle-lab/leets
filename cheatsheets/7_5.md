# Topic 7.5 · Quality Control, QA, TQM & Standards
**Specification Strand:** 7. Manufacturing Processes & Techniques
**Syllabus Focus:** Quality Control (QC), Quality Assurance (QA), Total Quality Management (TQM), ISO 9001, BSI Kitemark, CE / UKCA compliance

---

## QC vs QA & Total Quality Management

- **Quality Control (QC):** Reactive inspection of physical parts against engineering drawings and tolerances during and after production; defective items are rejected or reworked.
- **Quality Assurance (QA):** Proactive systemic management of processes, machine calibrations, and operator procedures to ensure defects cannot arise in the first place.
- **Total Quality Management (TQM):** Company-wide culture of continuous incremental improvement (Kaizen) where every employee is accountable for product excellence.
- **Statistical Process Control (SPC):** Control charts plotting critical part dimensions in real time; warns when tooling wear causes dimensions to drift before parts fall out of tolerance.

## Certification & Statutory Standards

- **ISO 9001:** International benchmark for Quality Management Systems; establishes documented procedures, calibrated inspection tooling, and regular audits.
- **BSI Kitemark:** Prestigious third-party certification from the British Standards Institution confirming independent testing and ongoing factory compliance.
- **CE and UKCA marking:** Mandatory conformity assessment marking declaring product adherence to all applicable safety, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), and low-voltage directives.
