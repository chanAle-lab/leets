# Topic 1.2 · Stakeholder Analysis & Enterprise
**Specification Strand:** 1. Identifying Requirements
**Syllabus Focus:** Stakeholder analysis, User-Centred Design (UCD), SWOT analysis, focus groups, market research, enterprise and routes to innovation

---

## User-Centred Design (UCD) & Investigation Methods

- **User-Centred Design (UCD):** An iterative design philosophy (ISO 9241-210) placing the explicit needs, cognitive capabilities, and physical ergonomics of end-users at every development phase.
- **SWOT analysis:** Systematic matrix mapping internal Strengths and Weaknesses against external Opportunities and Threats to guide strategic design engineering decisions.
- **Focus groups & qualitative observation:** Structured interactive sessions evaluating concept prototypes, aesthetic styling, tactile switch resistance, and feedback clarity.
- **Market research & gap analysis:** Quantitative demographic surveys, TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing, and identifying price-performance voids in current competitor product lines.

## Enterprise & Routes to Innovation

- **Entrepreneurship:** The commercial initiative and risk management required to translate technical inventions into viable market solutions.
- **Commercial partnerships & licensing:** Teaming with established Tier-1 manufacturers or licensing patented IP for royalties to leverage existing supply chains and global distribution.
- **Venture capital & angel investment:** Early-stage equity funding to finance high capital expenditure tooling (e.g. injection moulds, stamping dies) and CE/UKCA regulatory compliance testing.
- **Crowdfunding models:** Pre-sales platforms (e.g. Kickstarter) used to validate consumer demand, establish early product evangelists, and fund initial production runs without diluting equity.
