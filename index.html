OCR H404 Engineering Formulae Sheet (Appendices 5c & 5d)

Learners following Design Engineering (H404) must recall and apply these scientific and engineering formulae in written examinations (25% maths requirement):

1. Mechanics & Materials

Formula NameEquationVariables & Units
Young's Modulus Recall E = σ / ε E in Pa / GPa; σ = stress; ε = strain
Tensile / Compressive Stress Recall σ = F / A F = Force (N); A = Area (m²); σ in Pa (N/m²)
Tensile Strain Recall ε = ΔL / L₀ ΔL = Extension (m); L₀ = Original length (m)
Pressure Recall p = F / A p in N/m² (Pa); F in N; A in m²
Hooke's Law Recall F = k · x k = Spring constant (N/m); x = Extension (m)
Turning Effects / Torque Recall T = F · d (or M = F · x) T = Torque (N·m); F = Force (N); d = distance (m)
Gear Ratio (Velocity Ratio) GR = Teeth_driven / Teeth_driver = ω_in / ω_out Input/output rotational speeds (rpm / rad/s)
Mechanical Advantage & Efficiency MA = Load / Effort
η = (MA / VR) × 100%		 MA, VR dimensionless; η in percentage

2. Dynamics & Motion (SUVAT & Vectors)

ConceptFormulaNotes
SUVAT Equations v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as		 u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, s = displacement, t = time
Resolving Force Vectors F_x = F cos θ
F_y = F sin θ		 θ = angle with horizontal axis
Kinetic & Potential Energy E_k = ½ m v²
E_p = m g h		 g = 9.81 N/kg; m in kg, v in m/s, h in m
Work Done & Power W = F · d
P = W / t		 W in Joules (J); P in Watts (W)

3. Electricity & Electronics

Electrical LawEquationUnits
Ohm's Law Recall V = I · R V (Volts), I (Amperes), R (Ohms Ω)
Electrical Power Recall P = V · I = I²R = V² / R Power P in Watts (W)
Potential Divider V_out = V_in × [ R₂ / (R₁ + R₂) ] Voltage division across sensor networks
Energy Transferred E = P · t = V · I · t E in Joules (J) or kWh; t in seconds / hours
Wave Frequency & Period f = 1 / T f in Hz; T in seconds
