# Topic 4.2 · Digital Engineering Tools (CAD, CAE, FEA)
**Specification Strand:** 4. Design Thinking & Communication
**Syllabus Focus:** Parametric 3D CAD solid modelling, assembly mates, CAE simulation, Finite Element Analysis (FEA), CFD, digital twins

---

## Parametric CAD & Assembly Modeling

- **Parametric solid modelling:** Feature-based tree modeling (Extrude, Revolve, Sweep, Loft, Cut, Fillet) where dimensions drive geometry; altering a root sketch dynamically propagates across drawings and assemblies.
- **Kinematic assembly mates:** Coincident, concentric, parallel, tangent, and gear mechanical mates allowing digital verification of range of motion and collision detection.
- **DFM digital validation:** Draft angle inspection to ensure plastic injection mould release; wall thickness analysis to eliminate sink marks and internal voids.

## CAE, Finite Element Analysis (FEA) & Simulation

- **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** Discretising continuous solid models into a mesh of finite elements (tetrahedral/hexahedral) to calculate displacement and von Mises stress tensors under mechanical loads.
- **Stress concentration factors ($):** Detecting severe stress concentrations at sharp internal corners, keyways, and bolt holes; mitigated by adding generous transition fillets.
- **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD):** Simulating airflow velocity vectors, boundary layer separation, aerodynamic drag ($), and thermal convective heat dissipation across heatsinks.
