## Unit Cost Calculation & Breakeven Analysis

- **Unit Manufacturing Cost Formula:** $\\text{Unit Cost} = \\text{Direct Materials} + \\text{Direct Labour} + \\text{Overhead} + \\frac{\\text{Tooling \\& Development Cost}}{\\text{Total Production Volume}}$.
- **Tooling amortisation:** Distributing high upfront tooling costs (e.g. £50,000 for press dies) across unit volume; over 500,000 units tooling cost is £0.10/unit, while over 5,000 units it is £10.00/unit.
- **Breakeven Point (BEP):** $\\text{BEP (units)} = \\frac{\\text{Total Fixed Costs}}{\\text{Selling Price per Unit} - \\text{Variable Cost per Unit}}$.
- **Contribution Margin:** $\\text{Selling Price} - \\text{Variable Cost per Unit}$. The surplus generated per unit sold that contributes to paying off fixed overheads and generating net profit.
