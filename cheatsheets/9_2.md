# Topic 9.2 · Health & Safety Legislation (HASAW, COSHH)
**Specification Strand:** 9. Health & Safety
**Syllabus Focus:** Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, COSHH 2002 regulations, PPE Regulations 1992, CE/UKCA marking, product liability & warranties

---

## Statutory Health & Safety Legislation

- **Health and Safety at Work Act (HASAW) 1974:** Foundational UK statute placing a non-delegable legal duty on employers to ensure the health, safety, and welfare of employees and the public 'so far as is reasonably practicable'. Employees must take reasonable care of themselves and cooperate with safety protocols.
- **COSHH Regulations 2002:** Control of Substances Hazardous to Health; legally mandates comprehensive risk assessments, proper chemical storage in flame-proof bunded cabinets, Safety Data Sheets (SDS), and Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) testing for hazardous dusts, cutting fluids, and resin vapours.
- **PPE at Work Regulations 1992:** Mandates that employers must supply suitable, CE/UKCA-certified PPE (impact safety glasses EN 166, ear defenders EN 352, dust respirators, steel toe-cap footwear) free of charge wherever risks cannot be adequately controlled by engineering means.

## Product Safety Regulations & Consumer Warranties

- **General Product Safety Regulations (GPSR):** Prohibits placing any product on consumer markets that poses foreseeable safety hazards under reasonable usage or misuse.
- **Consumer Rights Act 2015:** Requires all engineered products sold to consumers to be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and match manufacturer descriptions; backed by mandatory warranty redress.
