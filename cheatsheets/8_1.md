# Topic 8.1 · Stakeholder Evaluation & Usability Testing
**Specification Strand:** 8. Viability of Design Solutions
**Syllabus Focus:** Usability trials, quantitative metrics, stakeholder feedback, accessibility compliance, testing against design specification

---

## Usability Trials & Stakeholder Verification

- **Structured usability trials:** Placing functional prototypes into the hands of target end-users to measure task completion duration, error frequency, and cognitive confusion.
- **Quantitative vs Qualitative data:** Quantitative metrics (seconds to complete action, clamping force applied in N, battery life in hours) vs qualitative observations (user comfort comments, intuitive grip feel).
- **Specification verification matrix:** Methodical check verifying every measurable requirement in the engineering technical specification against empirical prototype performance data.
