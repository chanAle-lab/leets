# Topic 8.2 · Technical Testing & Physical Validation
**Specification Strand:** 8. Viability of Design Solutions
**Syllabus Focus:** Destructive testing, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT: dye penetrant, ultrasonic, X-ray), environmental stress screening, FEA validation

---

## Destructive vs Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

- **Destructive testing:** Applying physical loads until catastrophic component failure occurs to determine ultimate mechanical limits: UTS tensile pull tests, burst pressure tests, drop impact tests.
- **Dye Penetrant Inspection (DPI):** NDT technique where fluorescent/red dye penetrates surface micro-cracks; developer draws dye out to highlight surface defects under white/UV light.
- **Ultrasonic Testing (UT):** High-frequency acoustic sound waves transmitted into solid components; reflected echo pulses detect internal sub-surface voids, inclusions, and delaminations.
- **X-Ray Radiography:** Penetrating ionizing radiation creates radiographic film images showing internal porosity in critical structural castings and welds.
