# Topic 9.1 · Workshop Safe Practices & Risk Assessments
**Specification Strand:** 9. Health & Safety
**Syllabus Focus:** Risk assessment matrix (likelihood × severity), hazard identification, hierarchy of control, machine interlocks, emergency stops

---

## Risk Assessment & Hazard Control

- **Hazard vs Risk:** A **hazard** is anything with intrinsic potential to cause harm (e.g. rotating lathe spindle, toxic solvent). A **risk** is the evaluated probability that harm occurs multiplied by its severity.
- **Risk Rating Formula:** $\\text{Risk Score} = \\text{Likelihood (1–5)} \\times \\text{Severity (1–5)}$. High scores require immediate implementation of higher-level engineering controls.
- **Hierarchy of Hazard Controls:** 1. **Elimination** (physically remove hazard), 2. **Substitution** (replace hazard with safer material), 3. **Engineering Controls** (interlocked guards, LEV local exhaust ventilation), 4. **Administrative Controls** (Standard Operating Procedures SOP, training, signage), 5. **PPE** (Personal Protective Equipment as last line of defence).

## Machinery Safety & Interlocking

- **Machine guarding:** Fixed guards preventing operator reach into entrapment pinch points; interlocked physical guards that immediately break the electrical control circuit and brake the drive spindle when opened.
- **Emergency stop circuits (E-Stop):** Latching mushroom-head push buttons wired into hardwired safety relays; must fail-safe to a de-energized state and require manual reset before machine restarts.
