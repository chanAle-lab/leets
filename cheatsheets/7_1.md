# Topic 7.1 · Iterative Models & Rapid Prototyping
**Specification Strand:** 7. Manufacturing Processes & Techniques
**Syllabus Focus:** Iterative prototyping, low-fidelity sketch models, test rigs, 3D printing (FDM, SLA), laser cutting, virtual simulation

---

## Iterative Physical & Digital Modelling

- **Low-fidelity sketch modelling:** Rapid construction using corrugated cardboard, Styrofoam, and wire to test volumetric proportions, visual ergonomics, and spatial clearance in hours.
- **Functional test rigs (breadboard / bread-rig):** Building isolated sub-system test benches (e.g. laser-cut acrylic gearboxes with microcontrollers) to validate physical kinematic forces before tooling.
- **Additive rapid prototyping (FDM / SLA):** Fused Deposition Modelling (FDM) extrudes molten thermoplastic filaments (PLA, ABS, PETG) layer-by-layer; Stereolithography (SLA) cures liquid photopolymer resin with UV lasers for ultra-smooth optical precision.
