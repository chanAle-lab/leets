## Systems Thinking & Sub-System Design

- **Systems thinking:** Holistic analysis focusing on interrelationships and feedback loops between constituent mechanical, electronic, and software sub-assemblies.
- **Three engineering sub-tasks:** 1. **User-Interface Design** (tactile feel, display ergonomics), 2. **Data Design** (signal protocols, memory budgets, bus bandwidth), 3. **Process Design** (state machines, loop timings).
- **Closed-loop vs open-loop:** Open-loop performs actions blindly without monitoring output (e.g. basic timer switch); closed-loop measures output via sensors and feeds back error signal to dynamically adjust actuators.

## Project Management & Quality Frameworks

- **Critical Path Analysis (CPA):** Network diagram calculation of Earliest Start Time (EST) and Latest Finish Time (LFT). The **Critical Path** is the sequence of dependent activities with zero float; any delay on this path delays the final delivery date.
- **Agile & Scrum:** Flexible development methodology organizing engineering teams into short 2–4 week sprints, daily stand-up meetings, and continuous prototype testing with stakeholders.
- **Six Sigma (DMAIC):** Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control. Statistical management methodology striving for fewer than 3.4 defective parts per million opportunities (.99966\\%$ yield).
