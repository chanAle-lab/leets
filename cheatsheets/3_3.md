## Nesting, Tessellation & Scrap Minimisation

- **2D Nesting algorithms:** CAD/CAM software mathematically optimizing part orientation and layout on standard sheet stock to maximize material yield.
- **Tessellation:** Geometrically interlocking identical profiles with common cutting edges, eliminating skeleton scrap and reducing laser/plasma cutting distance.
- **Scrap percentage formula:** $\\text{Scrap \\%} = \\frac{\\text{Total Raw Stock Area} - \\text{Total Useful Part Area}}{\\text{Total Raw Stock Area}} \\times 100\\%$.

## Standard Commercial Stock Forms

- **Stock forms:** Round bar, square bar, hexagonal bar, angle (L-section), channel (U-section), box section (SHS/RHS), I-beam, flat bar, tube, sheet and plate.
- **Cost advantages of standard stock:** Eliminates bespoke extrusion die setup costs, ensures immediate off-the-shelf availability, and guarantees certified mechanical properties.
