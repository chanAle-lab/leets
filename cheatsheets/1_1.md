# Topic 1.1 · Contexts for Design Solutions
**Specification Strand:** 1. Identifying Requirements
**Syllabus Focus:** Exploring contexts: environmental & surroundings, user requirements, economic & market considerations, product opportunities

---

## Context Dependency & Operating Surroundings

- **Context dependency:** All design practice is context dependent. An engineered solution cannot be validated in isolation; it must satisfy the precise physical, social, and operational conditions of its intended deployment.
- **Environmental and surroundings analysis:** Investigating extreme ambient temperatures, humidity, UV radiation exposure, vibration spectra, airborne particulate ingress (IP rating), chemical exposure, and installation envelope.
- **Economic and market constraints:** Analysing purchasing power of target demographics, competitor unit pricing benchmarks, target BOM (Bill of Materials) cost ceilings, and commercial return on investment (ROI).
- **Product opportunities:** Identifying market gaps, underserved user niches, regulatory shifts (e.g. decarbonisation mandates), or technological readiness improvements that enable novel solutions.

## Primary Users & Stakeholder Ecosystem

- **Primary user:** The direct operator interacting with the product's controls and user interface on a routine operational basis.
- **Wider stakeholders:** Maintenance technicians, installation teams, safety certifiers, logistics handlers, and recycling facilities whose operational requirements shape product architecture.
- **Contextual inquiry:** Direct ethnographic observation and semi-structured interviews in the real operational environment to reveal tacit needs that users rarely express in surveys.
