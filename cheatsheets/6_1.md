## Structural Reinforcement & Rigidity

- **Triangulation principle:** Triangles cannot be distorted without physically changing the length of one of their member struts; transforms complex bending forces into pure axial tension or compression.
- **Structural ribs and web stiffeners:** Thin perpendicular ribs integrated into injection mouldings and castings multiply the second moment of area ($) and section modulus ($), increasing bending stiffness by up to 10x with minimal material addition.
- **Gusset plates:** Welded or bolted triangular reinforcement plates securing column-to-beam joints to disperse concentrated bending moments over broader weld perimeters.
- **Monocoque vs space-frame:** Monocoque relies on a continuous structural skin (stressed skin) to absorb dynamic torsional and shear loads (e.g. aircraft fuselages); space-frame utilizes a three-dimensional triangulated skeletal truss.
