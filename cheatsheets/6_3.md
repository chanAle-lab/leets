## Stress, Strain & Elasticity Formulae (Recall Required)

- **Tensile / Compressive Stress ($\\sigma$):** $\\sigma = \\frac{F}{A}$ where $ is applied force in Newtons (N) and $ is original cross-sectional area in $\\text{m}^2$. Unit: Pascals ($\\text{Pa} = \\text{N/m}^2$,  \\text{ MPa} = 1 \\text{ N/mm}^2$).
- **Tensile Strain ($\\epsilon$):** $\\epsilon = \\frac{\\Delta L}{L_0}$ where $\\Delta L$ is change in length (extension) and $ is original length. Dimensionless ratio (often expressed as a percentage).
- **Young's Modulus of Elasticity ($):**  = \\frac{\\text{Stress}}{\\text{Strain}} = \\frac{\\sigma}{\\epsilon} = \\frac{F \\cdot L_0}{A \\cdot \\Delta L}$. Unit: $\\text{Pa}$ or $\\text{GPa}$ ( \\text{ GPa} = 10^9 \\text{ Pa}$). Constant gradient of linear elastic region of stress-strain curve.
- **Hooke's Law:**  = k \\cdot \\Delta x$ where $ is spring constant / stiffness in $\\text{N/m}$ and $\\Delta x$ is extension in metres.
- **Torque (Turning moment):** $\\text{Torque } T = F \\cdot d$ where $ is applied perpendicular force and $ is radial distance to pivot. Unit: $\\text{N}\\cdot\\text{m}$.

## SUVAT Equations & Modes of Mechanical Failure

- **SUVAT Equations of Motion:**  = u + at$;  = ut + \\frac{1}{2}at^2$; ^2 = u^2 + 2as$;  = \\frac{(u + v)t}{2}$.
- **Resolving Force Vectors:** Horizontal component  = F \\cos\\theta$; Vertical component  = F \\sin\\theta$.
- **Factor of Safety (FoS):** $\\text{FoS} = \\frac{\\text{Yield Strength or UTS}}{\\text{Allowable Working Stress}}$. Ensures structural integrity under unexpected peak overloads.
- **Modes of failure:** Elastic buckling (Euler buckling in slender compression struts), fatigue failure (cyclic alternating stress causing microscopic crack propagation), creep (gradual deformation under constant load at elevated temperatures).
