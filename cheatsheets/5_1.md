# Topic 5.1 · Factors Influencing Material Selection
**Specification Strand:** 5. Material & Component Considerations
**Syllabus Focus:** Selection criteria: functional performance, mechanical properties, cost, availability, machinability, environmental & ethical footprint

---

## Engineering Material Selection Factors

- **Functional requirements:** Mechanical load capacity, fatigue strength, operating temperature envelope, thermal expansion coefficient (CTE), corrosion resistance, electrical conductivity or dielectric breakdown.
- **Economic viability:** Balancing raw material cost per kg against machining speed, tooling amortisation, and scrap rate. (e.g. Aluminium costs more than steel per kg but machines 3x faster, reducing labour cost).
- **Ashby charts:** Material selection charts plotting property trade-offs (e.g. Young's Modulus vs Density /\\rho$, or Strength vs Cost $\\sigma/\\text{Cost}$) to identify optimal material candidates for minimum weight beams.
