# Topic 7.2 · Workshop Tools, Machining & Fabrication
**Specification Strand:** 7. Manufacturing Processes & Techniques
**Syllabus Focus:** Wasting (lathe turning, milling, drilling), joining (welding, brazing, soldering, fasteners), reforming (bending, vacuum forming), tolerances

---

## Subtractive Machining (Wasting)

- **Centre lathe operations:** Cylindrical turning: facing off datum ends, parallel turning, taper turning, boring, knurling, and parting off. Work held in 3-jaw self-centring chuck or between centres.
- **Vertical milling machine:** Multi-tooth rotating cutter traversing workpiece in X, Y, and Z axes to create flat faces, steps, slots, pockets, and keyways.
- **Drilling & reaming:** Twist drill generates rough hole; machine reamer achieves exact dimensional diameter and mirror surface finish within ISO tolerance limits.

## Fabrication, Joining & Reforming

- **Welding:** Parent metals melted and fused with filler material. MIG (Metal Inert Gas; wire feed, argon shielding, fast structural steel); TIG (Tungsten Inert Gas; tungsten electrode, highest aesthetic quality on aluminium/stainless steel).
- **Brazing & Soldering:** Non-ferrous filler metal melted below parent metal melting points ($>450°\\text{C}$ for brazing; $<450°\\text{C}$ for electrical lead-free soldering).
- **Mechanical fasteners & locking:** ISO metric bolts (M3, M4, M5); Nyloc locking nuts and Belleville disc springs prevent fastener loosening under vibration.
- **Sheet metal folding:** Sheet metal folded along bend lines using workshop box-and-pan folder, accounting for bend allowance and neutral axis K-factor.
