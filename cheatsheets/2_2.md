# Topic 2.2 · Technological Developments & Innovation
**Specification Strand:** 2. Existing Products & Practice
**Syllabus Focus:** Emerging technologies, blue sky innovation vs incremental innovation, disruptive technologies, smart systems, Industry 4.0

---

## Innovation Types & Technology Lifecycles

- **Incremental innovation:** Successive, low-risk refinements to existing products (e.g. shaving 5% component weight, increasing battery energy density by 3%, firmware updates).
- **Disruptive / Blue sky innovation:** Radically novel technology that displaces entrenched markets or creates entirely new industries (e.g. solid-state batteries, generative AI-driven design, electric vehicle skateboards).
- **Technology Readiness Levels (TRL):** NASA/EU scale from TRL 1 (basic principles observed) to TRL 9 (proven in full operational commercial environment).

## Emerging Engineering Technologies

- **Additive metal manufacturing (DMLS):** Direct Metal Laser Sintering enabling complex generative internal cooling channels impossible via subtractive milling.
- **Internet of Things (IoT) edge computing:** Integrating low-power sensors and microcontrollers with cloud connectivity for real-time predictive maintenance telemetry.
- **Advanced composites & meta-materials:** Tailored carbon-nanotube matrices and auxetic structures exhibiting negative Poisson's ratio for ultra-high impact absorption.
