# Topic 7.4 · Scales of Production & Lean / JIT
**Specification Strand:** 7. Manufacturing Processes & Techniques
**Syllabus Focus:** Production scales (one-off, batch, mass, continuous), cellular manufacturing, Lean manufacturing (7 wastes), Just-in-Time (JIT)

---

## Scales of Manufacturing Production

- **One-off / bespoke production:** Highly customized single units manufactured by skilled artisans or specialist machinists; flexible, high unit cost, minimal tooling.
- **Batch production:** Pre-determined batch sizes using flexible machine setups and interchangeable jigs/fixtures; balances setup downtime against warehouse storage inventory.
- **Mass / High-volume production:** Dedicated automated assembly lines producing millions of standardized identical products; ultra-low unit cost, immense capital investment in dedicated tooling.
- **Continuous flow production:** 24/7 automated continuous processing of bulk materials (oil refining, steel blast furnaces, petrochemicals).

## Lean Manufacturing & Just-In-Time (JIT)

- **Lean manufacturing:** Systematic elimination of the 7 wastes (TIMWOOD): Transport, Inventory, Motion, Waiting, Overproduction, Over-processing, Defects.
- **Just-In-Time (JIT):** Components and raw stock arrive at assembly lines exactly as needed; eliminates buffer warehouse inventory and minimizes tied-up working capital.
- **Cellular manufacturing:** U-shaped workstations manned by cross-trained technicians, minimizing part transport distance and optimizing operator workflow balance.
