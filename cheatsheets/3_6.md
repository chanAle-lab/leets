# Topic 3.6 · Cross-Disciplinary STEM in Engineering
**Specification Strand:** 3. Implications of Wider Issues
**Syllabus Focus:** Applying mathematics, physics, and materials science to engineering decisions, interpreting technical datasheets and curves

---

## STEM Integration & Datasheet Analysis

- **Applied mathematics in engineering:** Trigonometric vector resolution for truss analysis, calculus for dynamic rate of change, and statistics for manufacturing quality control.
- **Physics principles:** Conservation of energy, Newton's laws of motion, Faraday's law of induction, thermodynamics of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation).
- **Interpreting technical datasheets:** Deriving maximum continuous drain current ($), thermal junction-to-ambient resistance ($\\theta_{JA}$), and stress-strain curves to ensure components operate within their Safe Operating Area (SOA).
