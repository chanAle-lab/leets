## Ohm's Law, Power & Potential Dividers

- **Ohm's Law:**  = I \\cdot R$; Potential difference (Volts) = Current (Amperes) × Resistance (Ohms).
- **Electrical Power:**  = V \\cdot I = I^2 R = \\frac{V^2}{R}$ [Watts, W].
- **Potential Divider Equation:** {out} = V_{in} \\cdot \\frac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$. Fundamental circuit to convert sensor resistance changes into readable voltage signals.
- **Resistor network rules:** Series:  = R_1 + R_2 + \\dots$; Parallel: $\\frac{1}{R_T} = \\frac{1}{R_1} + \\frac{1}{R_2} + \\dots$

## Sensors, Semiconductor Switching & Actuators

- **Sensors:** LDR (light-dependent resistor; resistance falls with light intensity), NTC Thermistor (negative temperature coefficient; resistance drops as temperature rises), Piezoelectric sensor, Hall effect (magnetic proximity).
- **MOSFET as high-current electronic switch:** N-channel MOSFET turns fully ON when Gate-Source voltage exceeds threshold ({GS} > V_{th}$). Minimal gate current; ultra-low {DS(on)}$ eliminates resistive heat losses in motor control.
- **Flyback protection diode:** Placed in reverse parallel across inductive loads (motors, solenoids, relays) to clamp dangerous back-EMF voltage spikes generated during switch-off.
- **Actuators:** Brushed DC motors (continuous rotary motion, speed controlled by PWM), Servo motors (closed-loop angular positioning 0–180°), Stepper motors (discrete digital step angles for CNC positioning).
