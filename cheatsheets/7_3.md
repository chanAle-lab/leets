# Topic 7.3 · Industrial Commercial Manufacture & SMT
**Specification Strand:** 7. Manufacturing Processes & Techniques
**Syllabus Focus:** Commercial polymer moulding (injection, blow, compression, thermoforming), metal casting (sand, die), stamping, SMT assembly

---

## Commercial Polymer Moulding Methods

- **Injection moulding:** Molten polymer forced under extreme hydraulic pressure (up to 1500 bar) into hardened tool steel mould cavities. High tool cost (£15k–£100k+), ultra-fast cycle times (10–30s), complex geometries, integrated snap fits.
- **Blow moulding:** Molten hollow plastic parison extruded, clamped in mould, and inflated with compressed air; produces seamless bottles, containers, and fuel tanks.
- **Compression moulding:** Preheated thermosetting charge placed into open heated cavity, closed under hydraulic pressure; produces heat-resistant electrical plugs and melamine tableware.

## Industrial Metal Casting & Electronics SMT Assembly

- **High pressure die casting:** Molten aluminium or zinc alloy injected under high pressure into permanent steel dies; excellent surface finish, thin walls, tight dimensional tolerances.
- **Sand casting:** Molten metal poured into temporary sand moulds formed around patterns; low tooling cost, ideal for large engine blocks and low volume castings.
- **Surface Mount Technology (SMT) Assembly:** 1. Laser-cut stainless steel stencil applies solder paste onto PCB surface pads, 2. High-speed pick-and-place robot places surface-mount components, 3. Multi-zone reflow oven melts solder paste at ~217°C to form robust solder joints.
