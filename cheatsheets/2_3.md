# Topic 2.3 · Historical Movements & Influential Engineers
**Specification Strand:** 2. Existing Products & Practice
**Syllabus Focus:** Design movements (Arts & Crafts, Bauhaus, Modernism, Postmodernism/Memphis), influential figures (Rams, Dyson, Brunel), social & environmental legacy

---

## Major Design & Engineering Movements

- **Arts & Crafts (1880–1920):** William Morris; emphasis on handcrafted integrity, honest use of natural materials, and moral rejection of dehumanising industrial mass manufacture.
- **Bauhaus (1919–1933):** Walter Gropius; 'Form follows function', bridging art with industrial manufacturing, geometric purity, tubular steel, glass, and modular architecture.
- **Modernism & International Style:** Mies van der Rohe ('Less is more'); structural steel, reinforced concrete, clean unadorned surfaces, standardization.
- **Memphis Group (1981–1988):** Ettore Sottsass; colourful rebellion against cold functionalism, combining geometric whimsy, patterned laminates, and provocative postmodern aesthetics.

## Influential Design Engineers & Philosophy

- **Dieter Rams:** Chief design officer at Braun; 'Good design is as little design as possible'. Established the iconic 10 Principles of Good Design emphasizing utility, honesty, unobtrusiveness, and longevity.
- **Sir James Dyson:** Cyclonic air separation technology; executed 5,127 functional prototypes to eliminate vacuum bags, embodying persistence through empirical test rigs.
- **Isambard Kingdom Brunel:** Victorian engineering giant; pioneered screw-propelled iron steamships (SS Great Britain) and audacious suspension bridges, calculating structural limits with innovative ironwork.
