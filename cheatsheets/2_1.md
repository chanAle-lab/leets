# Topic 2.1 · Product Analysis & Evaluation
**Specification Strand:** 2. Existing Products & Practice
**Syllabus Focus:** Product analysis, disassembly, ACCESS FM, materials, construction methods, functionality, ergonomics, branding & commercial viability

---

## Systematic Product Disassembly & Reverse Engineering

- **Product disassembly:** Methodical physical teardown of existing market products to analyse component count, mechanical sub-systems, fastening choices, and manufacturing workflows.
- **ACCESS FM evaluation:** Aesthetics, Cost, Customer, Environment, Size, Safety, Function, Material. Standard evaluative framework for rigorous product deconstruction.
- **Bill of Materials (BOM) analysis:** Cataloguing each discrete part, material type, net weight, standard fastener specifications (e.g. DIN 912 screws), and estimated unit production costs.

## Fitness for Purpose & Benchmarking

- **Fitness for purpose:** Objective engineering verification that a product meets all functional, environmental, and lifespan criteria specified in its design brief under operational duty cycles.
- **Branding and aesthetic identity:** Evaluating surface finishes, colourways, haptics, and brand coherence that drive consumer quality perception.
- **Enhancement opportunities:** Identifying structural fatigue vulnerabilities, excessive thermal buildup, redundant parts, or difficult assembly operations ripe for value-engineering redesign.
