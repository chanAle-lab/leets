# Topic 4.1 · 2D & 3D Sketching, Drawing & Modelling
**Specification Strand:** 4. Design Thinking & Communication
**Syllabus Focus:** Sketching, 3rd angle orthographic projection (BS 8888), section views, isometric, circuit & system block diagrams, flowcharts

## BS 8888 Engineering Drawing Standards

- **Third Angle Orthographic Projection:** Universal standard engineering drawing layout: Plan view positioned directly above Front elevation; End elevation positioned on the side viewed.
- **Line conventions (BS 8888):** Continuous thick (visible edges), Continuous thin (dimension, leader, and projection lines), Dashed thin (hidden details), Chain thin (centre lines, axes of symmetry).
- **Sectional views:** Hatched at 45° to reveal interior bores and chambers. Standard rule: webs, ribs, shafts, bolts, nuts, and pins are **never hatched** in longitudinal section.
- **Dimensioning conventions:** Dimensions placed above horizontal dimension lines or to the left of vertical lines; arrowheads sharp and filled; leader lines never crossing dimension lines.

## System Visualisations & Schematic Diagrams

- **System block diagrams:** High-level signal flow decomposition: Input Sensor -> Conditioning Circuit -> Microcontroller Process -> Driver Stage -> Mechanical Actuator.
- **Circuit schematics:** Standardized electronic component symbols (resistors, NPN/MOSFET transistors, diodes, capacitors) showing logical electrical connectivity.
- **Flowcharts:** Program logic flow; Terminals (oval), Operations/Processes (rectangle), Input/Output (parallelogram), Decisions/Conditionals (diamond).
